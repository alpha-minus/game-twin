# Game Twin

[![Game Twin Beta Review](https://img.youtube.com/vi/pVEn2ZNQklo/0.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pVEn2ZNQklo)

**A second-screen companion for guides, maps, images, checklists and achievements.**

Going for 100% means having a walkthrough, a collectible map, a missables list and your own checklist all within reach at once. Then you put the game down for a week, or switch to something else, and it's gone: the guide tab is closed, the map isn't where you left it, the checklist is buried three folders deep.

Game Twin gives every game its own vault. It finds that material for you, files it with next to no effort, and keeps every saved copy readable offline. Switch games and your whole workspace switches with it, exactly where you left off.

Free, ad-free, no analytics or trackers.

## What you get

- **Guides.** One tap to a walkthrough for the game you are on, from StrategyWiki, GameFAQs, IGN, Neoseeker and Steam Community. Where a series has a better home than the general sites, it goes there instead: Metroid to Omega Metroid, Zelda to Zelda Dungeon, Pokémon to Bulbapedia, Fire Emblem to Fire Emblem WoD, curated title by title rather than guessed from a search. Reader mode, offline saving, PDF, TXT, EPUB, HTML and MHT, bookmarks, find-in-page and automatic table of contents.
- **Images and maps.** Maps from VGMaps, from the series sites that publish their own, and from image search. Add your own markers, and the app remembers pan and zoom per image. Screenshot capture saves straight into the vault.
- **Achievements.** RetroAchievements and Steam in one view, with missable and unlocked filters and offline caching. Unlocks replay as an animated timeline, and you can put other players on it to compare.
- **Checklists.** Collectibles, side quests and boss strategies, color-tagged, reorderable, importable from markdown.
- **Search that already knows the game.** The game's name is added to your query automatically, so something vague like "third heart piece" lands on the right page the first time instead of the wrong game's wiki. Your last ten searches, your open tabs and your saved pages all stay attached to that game, so picking a run back up is picking up the thread, not retyping it. Multi-tab browser with reader mode and optional ad, tracker and cookie-banner blocking.
- **AI assistant.** Optional, and off until you enter your own Google Gemini key. Ask about the spot you are stuck on, with a screenshot of the top screen if you want one attached.
- **Library and sync.** Artwork-rich grid and list views, external storage, and sync through Syncthing, DriveSync or any folder-sync app.
- **Apps.** Install emulators and companion apps directly or through Obtainium, arranged for the second screen.

Full feature list and screenshots: **[game-twin.app](https://game-twin.app)**

### Devices

Works on any Android 13+ phone or tablet. On handhelds it picks up your recent games from ES-DE, Cocoon, RetroArch, RetroAchievements and Steam, with full controller support. On dual-screen handhelds such as the AYN Thor the companion panel lives on the second screen while you play on the first, which is what the app was built around.

## Getting started

1. Install the APK from [Releases](https://github.com/alpha-minus/game-twin/releases/latest), or add the repo to Obtainium with the badge above.
2. Pick a folder for your files.
3. Optional: add RetroAchievements or Steam credentials, or point Settings at your ES-DE, Cocoon or RetroArch folder, for automatic game detection.
4. Launch a game. Game Twin creates the library entry for you.

## Privacy

No analytics, no ads, no behavioural tracking, no user account, and no server operated by me. Your library, guides, maps, checklists, images and any credentials you enter stay on your device and in the folders you choose to sync.

The app does reach the network for things you ask it to do: artwork and metadata, the RetroAchievements and Steam APIs with credentials you supply, GitHub for updates, the built-in browser, and Google Gemini if you switch the AI assistant on with your own key.
