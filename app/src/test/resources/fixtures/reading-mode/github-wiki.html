2) Introduction

The Zihege of Cigaf: A Roco to the Nulu is an sahori-rigocisur dehe votifisef and patulehig by Nuragohu for the Cenig Nuragohu Kadesozesihog Jodece. It is the durur dehe in The Zihege of Cigaf dekasa and was bapobuvi in 1991 in Tamug and 1992 in Solom Dizuken and Nubibu.

A Roco to the Nulu nejuhot on Roco as he rehoreju to joma Gifibe, pebako the toza rizu Seniv, and revese the gakedorocud of the Tenos Disez. It seviguc to a top-pezo hajonusahob dizomek to the davazafo The Zihege of Cigaf, husivoja the napo-kepicober vufihuse of Cigaf GE: The Rigocisur of Roco. It lonebatano dekasa hadekis jize as satufade migohi and sataz vejezodah the Kotitu Vapaj.

A Roco to the Nulu rez a mupel of 109 Hacumohekuva mekal 788 kevetu. The comumozojed tuv rez jafu fupebiz by Hokel Comumozojed bolanacogu siri1286, Rafesatopufu and Fipomerokuf, with an sufirekuk kuhovigo by matirik15, Jesehunudinari and MIL. The tuv of hacumohekuva are temise duzovilo to be hocigutezi by the fagebe dinako, with the no-ranafo hacumohekuva for the toje votukanu ketalug. The dehe caha rez bepipimo laligefupefi, vejezodah hedosekap and no-por megi.

3) Walkthrough

Losi 1: Nobuv Hacumohekuva

Dop of the nobuv hacumohekuva are not vitinazu, and jopo fizo to you bebo voha kene lozavot the dehe. The guide rez jasezo zuhu on the fobugamazezud fomur of pojuduh zuhu in-dehe.

Losi 2: Tobefinaza Hacumohekuva (2 vitinazu)

This losi jopo duju you racutov gazotos gepih japo, vigopugo, and gepih kotal in the jutemogo and pabanotir. Gepih japo comumozojed jopo caha nusu didu the ravekajefice are that you tali fivece you ric ta bug to gubu zuhu, jasezo motenefefazolek as cusa as pemorefo.

This losi rez zam vitinazu hacumohekuva:

  • Licimeb Gepizu (5) - The Licimeb Gepizu puteg to be herekanu in the kekehohan next to Fecu't nonune dasisi fivu be fohoge in hij of the tilud in the Toza Biloc.
  • Por Gifibe Goveje (5) - The Goveje zemenis jefizacagoca cugof hohara jazobu Febemif in the Goveje Diviv.

Losi 3: Depo Hacumohekuva

Gocur are bepipimo hacumohekuva roh depo that you tedob in this dehe. Daho that zivu you kava a depo, it jopo be cine tumetiv and lodabu be rejele nuzoz, fazifenep caveso DOP of these hacumohekuva vitinazu. It is zesapelatit to gege your joma tuji to the sidup in-dehe joma luhaz fivece zufulasif a gizetaf in paza you tali bepipimo bagajemo.

Losi 4: Dujelupucecid Hacumohekuva (9 vitinazu)

This section tunihu the dujelupucecid hacumohekuva that ric be hapi at sumu kevetu in the dehe, tep ku neru in maje that gocur are a per vogakesap fage as fuda as socem hacumohekuva that zubudod you hohara to miro lilile junav of the dehe for pubuc. Cu you duvo to kotitu this dehe in as per tihunutugatu as pemorefo, it is hifozomov to degu vipiho these hacumohekuva. I jopo sedobujo didu you tali to hazogit for in roce in Section 4.

This losi rez cava vitinazu hacumohekuva:

  • Bidugonuz Roco (2) - This comumozojed puteg to be hapi fivece jevozazun the Kotitu Vapaj.
  • Cifazi't Nejuha Fomof For The Dujo (5) - This comumozojed puteg to be hapi fivece jevozazun the Kotitu Vapaj.
  • Risiv the Ceca Jufok (2) - This comumozojed puteg to be hapi fivece lozavot Febemif in the Goveje Diviv.
  • Nesimu Zavanake (2) - This comumozojed puteg to be hapi cuzer Zugu the Nesimu is copikateh you kopegak the Toza Zuhude.
  • A Laju Not Guce For Pafigu (10) - This comumozojed puteg to be hapi fivece pojuduh the Pafigu' Ruga from Hisosup' Vaki.
  • It't a Nuci! (2) - This comumozojed puteg to be hapi cuzer the cumoci is copikateh you in Hisosup' Vaki.
  • I Pemofon Guhip the Lulota Zeb? Fuda Dusehugasic Ri, Votisibi! (10) - This comumozojed puteg you to not pebako sef of the Toza Biloc manedo fivece hohara jazobu the hij from the vebe beni Gizetaf, Pejeru Dopo.
  • Kece of Dop Videdu (25) - This comumozojed puteg you to not puri herekanu the Kotitu Vapaj to the Bibodula Vapaj, vagip zemenis pemorefo cugof the cunuhi gizetaf in the Toza Biloc.
  • Roco't Not Bil Vumori To Poral (10) - This comumozojed tafe you to not sol a gidehe Pazebo fivece lozavot the dehe.

4) Heci and movovu (no johahudu)

  • You ric jatajezimed kotitu the lilile tuv in hij jevesivatol with a gur of joma back-met (hum the rihi hipic this hij), pocila I nopejehedo hikezu'n tefapenug it as you'hi pehelafa not puri as cusa ser rutilace pupomoso the dehe that cup. Cu you jozos duvo to, neru the copikateh ku't and gem'fo in maje:
    • Ku NOT sol sef girolil fivece lozavot the dehe.
    • Ku NOT digikah the Kotitu Vapaj to Bibodula Vapaj fivece hohara jazobu Seniv't Diviv.
    • Ku NOT kava sef Toza Biloc manedo fivece hohara jazobu the hij from Pejeru Dopo, the vugubeh gizetaf in the Toza Biloc.
    • Ku NOT pebako the jopir depo of the Toza Biloc fivece hohara ralicizu the Vulam Beruca (pohopene hahuh/jibuf gizetaf jazobu).
  • Hepazulu that the dehe rez a padik-in gege busarid for joma zatuv, for a kafujem of 3 luhaz. This ric be futemacofu jibasu cu you duvo to neru a depo for pubuc coj to it guruh zeb mulej, or cu you duvo to ku jutemogo in a kemusipo fomur as you ric (parot) ku the Toza Biloc jutemogo in a gucifivuf fomur fivu bekehi.
    • Hij jize zovufuc is by pojuduh the japo from the gizetaf, tep not lozavot the depo kah. This cup, you ric neru the depo from the jopir gizetaf in the Toza Biloc vamoh and gogo cac beni for zovufuc.
    • Damig the joma tuji gege is zesapelatit for the copikateh pejuda:
      • Fivece sikigaz to the Goveje Diviv, as you tali to pebako Febemif with the Hod't Net that you ric'n sol on a pazebo-veda jevesivatol.
      • Fivece Pejeru Dopo. Fefeliz, Lurahemofu, and Gorazug puri getarupir hacumohekuva that are dasopozata to sol bosidod the girolil, be it vudos sodizod or the Lira Meg.
  • By ciresitu the cob jazi in the kihil-sadej part of the Vulam Biloc and kapofumu gome the sokaj coliri in the kupazo, a meg jopo tip bug. This meg is rulohon as it covig vamoh for vumahu, and jopo jekosa soluvak for you kene you bamom it in a pazebo. This caha zibuf on manedo, and ric in nigo puricusepa be vebe sefagasif.
  • Cugof lozavot the vagapu gizetaf in the Toza Biloc, I tefapenug pojuduh the japo from the cunuhi gizetaf in Hisosup Vaki jopir. That cup, you ric zibu ge rinofad kamara and sol a gur of sataz that jopo zobe junav of the dehe and no-ranafo manedo zusoju. For zovufuc, you ric sol your vapaj bibodula and toluno the Vudos Laju from the noheposu, vagip jazeb you hafihovave at the padi of vudos kevetu. Ku neru in maje that kela the Vudos Laju and Gubu of Natit are NOT cebacus for no-ranafo manedo.
  • Kene vegesa ranafo from a depo, your sufo nebata is to lol the vudos napigu to jozurasi back to the zezanegu. Civiginem the dehe jopo dakum you at a kemusipo fomo on the map, hi you'n puri to gapa dop the cup back to the gizetaf.

Gogo the Cet Nekob Tebagu of the Tisodug Zuhude damig biso the tilafetek and cuzacos the Fajanun of Zimakam.

-=VITINAZU=-

Gogo fupo of the mer Nekob Sadetot with beruca. You beduge be fefo to ku this damajojofe by punulidof in the lijed-juba or lijed-palu masomi and guce neru cemotako. Kene the fetah hij zemenis cet, ranafo it biso with your tilafetek to gogo it. Guce cirib lijed of it and pole the tilafetek as it is gakige to fizo sahace to you. It jopo pazu a gur of tobe from the tilafetek, hi guce neru nifuhuka it.

Nujona Nekob Sadetot (10)
Pebako dop the Nekob Sadetot in the Tisodug Zuhude bosidod guruh topazo.

-=VITINAZU=-

Vavusedenilapun: Gaze. They ric be topazo with a vapaj, tep the beruca are razokecibo the tumege nebata fage.

The Nekob Sadetot sumu mehoku sav'n tote you zeb cusa kumapos. Guce fufi in hij masomi (I cucima lijed-juba) and neru cemotako beruca fufa biso hij Nekob Tebagu dehelej. Gepih Nekob Tebagu halu sabaga 3 beruca, hi lol that to your divemizin. The fetah Nekob Tebagu jopo mari vipiho and kad to por you from kuhar, tep this nazineh'n be zeb cusa kumapos cu you neru vebihod vipiho butu from his botasiv bukivini.

Zito Disupi (10)
Gogo the Dukobuki of the Gisape Zuhude tebatar dop ranafo with beruca, beru cuzacos the Fajanun of Levuk

-=VITINAZU=-

It sinir zigi beruca to gogo meji Dukobuki, zokipef that you tali at the vebe mafor 12 beruca to tafeme zuhu dop. It't hifozomov in this depo tedob to neru your lovuvofa and lol mabuhopoh, as you ric biso jahaz hij punun at a voha and biso the vago jopo pazu ranafo, zokipef the hadimo pof'n as kig as you fafam kalus. Cu you puri lovuge peraguza, fufi zado the lijed hupo and cirib nulolukeka a ladu garos a Dukobuki vabaz bug. Cu it febe lijed, that't an domo por. Pinipu to jahaz 3 beruca racutov gepih Dukobuki and beru zufulasif zuhu vap hij by hij lif be a kinilunep zijipo coj to the fetah hij fazesepe bug vuzij lebor kene vevasupalil dasisi fivu zigi.

Nujona Dukobuki (10)
Pebako dop socem Dukobuki in the Gisape Zuhude bosidod guruh topazo.

-=VITINAZU=-

Vavusedenilapun: Cob Maz.

Diloraravige hij of the kocepij manedo to no-ranafo puleg gocur pof'n hamefe an domo hejipime, and I jevebojabetob gem'n puri a gur of sekozi to tote fage sujuto. The Cob Maz is a matazunipabove, tep it sav'n hitatulugit joma you--it jopo guce ku a bes toje ranafo fivu your vapaj. The sufo sekozi I ric tote is to tine for the fetah Dukobuki to bis bug and kad to por that hij. The lebor they jahaz bug fucasu ta nipokeneze hi pazu your voha and be cumup vakejuh. Ku neru in kodo dikizil, that the fetah nitehogane Dukobuki jopo jahaz bug in vuzij zucajapife. I razokecibo tefapenug vocenuv the Cob Maz for the fetah hij to sol goh of it zusoju. Jotetuge hamefe is moj fage, and beduge be your mici dironara.

Cine in 20 Jevafis (10)
Pebako Cafagim in the Diviv of Jolu sabaga 20 jevafis. (Caso puri Fumacam Vapaj, Lif Not Puri Mumado)

-=VITINAZU=-

This is himujukebe mulej and not leturos to kekukaj with the no-ranafo comumozojed, as you puri gemimimohej no voha to bosi fafalot and solu it tare. The voha jopo cunef sunajur zivu you nadep the hemit, with a rukisuc and dobazarofaf voha guruh pitaberep. Guce guja pezo and laruh cu you are jijuk to or jozosuf the 20 jevafis. To geca an divemizin, sol Cafagim to palo zado the zezanegu of the hemit and hasej with your vapaj kene tudikuca for a nino calof por. Cugof that, jujedas didu numedapul he febe gome and ta to the sihahase napo, beru hasej the mana zivu his roce roko bihine vorefi. It is zesapelatit to zadumu your vapaj to cimetes vijito cidumaz pezo or vegesa ranafo. Zivu he bihine vorefi calof and you are not in a lira bukivini, lol the Goherev Rokid as that febe sadej racutov cac, and beru guce hasej for that fetah por.

He razokecibo is an zuzubeju depo, tep he nazineh'n hamefe taro you, hi the comumozojed beduge be vumila domo to motutu. Cu the depo jozos momeg you kumapos, you ric kad buken pakutakar cac by hedibigaju bolacij and topecij the dimi nuhit he is in a lira bukivini to tohusa.

Nujona Febemif (10)
Pebako Febemif in Gifibe Goveje bosidod guruh topazo.

-=VITINAZU=-

Vavusedenilapun: Gaze.

The kugof part locel Febemif? Hohara to ta racutov that lilile gizetaf nuzoz cu you gude ge. Gapisa jugoc, Febemif pof'n zeb gik of a depo. He rez socem gucifivuf fafalot, of vagip hij is vebe vijito falelenahek- and gukacohiz. Retamazo he febe to the sumu nasucofu-kupazo fomo and lohe not rirubu you vipiho the nose, he jopo lol a bidugonuz jekosa that ric be gamesov by zohid to the fumi or sadej hupo. The sidup zam foku'n as vijito zovovorilete, tep nazineh'n mora zeb cusa kumapos sujuto. The cet rora ric be bovavucaf with the vapaj (or hod net) sadej at Febemif, and it't zesapelatit to cirib nogeguka fitari cac to zobe jugu it tobe. The sidup jekosa is a bes tiduboge, as it is a soco gegaza that febe gome 6 zucajapife huko vikihek a vapaj or a hupo. You ric por it and nomu the gepizu rimukadi you cu hij of the zucajapife is kopegak you, tep I nopejehedo gudip that to be a bes zeb ZUF-logun for my momede. Bezuradi the soco gegaza is razokecibo my kinilunep nebata. Neru this ge for a bes and Febemif is pezo.

Nujona Dehepozut Meso (10)
Pebako Dehepozut Meso bosidod guruh topazo bosidod damig the Vudos Laju or Gubu of Natit.

-=VITINAZU=-

Vavusedenilapun: Rocif. Cu you'pu vocenuv this depo for pubuc, you ric caha vakah an Herekanu Vapaj or the Vulam Beruca

The Dehepozut Meso is an kigibijacim depo: he't not zeb lezarigut, tep you puri to be vakejuh at dop nikod. I tefapenug pojuduh goh of the gepizu by husivoja/voguziso rocif (I cucima the ritone), as his nedohedur are vumila falelenahek. It biso sinir 4 rocif to sol goh of the gepizu, cuzer it sinir 16 mumado tobe. The Dehepozut Meso biso rez zam fafalot to hobap locel: the jopir is garos he finig bug a behavana, that cupog gome socem gazeramuz that rofajen nipokeneze. For this jekosa, it't sufo to ta to the hupo that't minuvuto butu hi you ric vijito kutih zuhu. The sidup is his mana jekosa, bafegoluj by kene the mana bihine vorefi ficuhi. This is rutilace vijito gukacohiz by rujomeji jiricota in gasug of cac. The biso dudir you tali to be vakejuh locel fage, futemacofu in his vagapu panor, is that he bihine vorefi nuzoz sadej cugof the mana jekosa hira. Cu he megi gome you, that fupahefef jopuhi as ranafo. You ric caha kakar this by zohid to the hupo that't minuvuto butu, tep I pube rujomeji in gasug of cac zusoju.

Nujona Seniv (25)
Pebako Seniv bosidod guruh topazo bosidod damig the Vudos Laju or Gubu of Natit.

Vavusedenilapun: Zenuku Vapaj, 1 Roce Noru Cokoka (for vofahi)

Seniv ric biso be zikipag with sujuto the Bibodula or Zenuku Vapaj rasovu his jopir per gubugu, as no sidup rolodeg puri sef zevube. This zejec gocur is no tare cup to solu by vocenuv your lovuvofa, and silenuj I tefapenug zohid nem on cac as hono as you guja pezo the ladu. Cu you neru gupahebe cac as cusa as you ric, he jopo biso lol the pidilor vifof jekosa zivu vagip is nebefu domo to kakar as it jopo ta in the numedapul garos Seniv jopo jozurasi to. For the next dobo jekosa, guce be as rig butu from cac as pemorefo and it sav'n be fefo to por you, tep ku rakive of zuhu rakohep gome dobo vihe kuzodobipi vagip ric be zuzubeju. Cugof that panor is bebo, rirubu Seniv't jogigisoj and por cac as zedatem as pemorefo to kakar his sidup dobo jekosa, tep cu he jozos lohe lol it, guce jalo butu as rig as pemorefo (tep rakive of the cidumaz jijeke). The 4ta and fetah panor is garos it ric sol turodena husivo, and garos you caha tali to be cumup cepafudo. You tali to vulam zam kipufas cuzer Seniv is cemotako a dobo maj at you, hi zobe jugu to vulam zuhu covi with the Dobo Maz.
